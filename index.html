01Desktop · 2026

Rask

A native chat for Ollama. Ask a local model. If that model can use tools, it searches the web itself.

One window and a tray icon. Each model keeps its own system prompt. Close hides the window. After it sits idle, the model is unloaded from memory. The download is a Windows installer.

02Desktop · 2026

PiLoad

A desktop app for yt-dlp. Download locally, or send the job to a DietPi Raspberry Pi over SSH.

Paste one video URL per line and pick a quality. For a local download, PiLoad fetches yt-dlp, FFmpeg and Deno the first time they are needed. On the Pi, the files stay on the Pi.

03Userscript · 2026

ezInstaDL

A Violentmonkey userscript that places discreet save buttons on Instagram photos, videos, carousels, Reels, and Stories.

One click saves the visible item; carousels can be saved as a set. It runs entirely in the browser, prefers the full-size URL already loaded for your session, and stays out of the way.
